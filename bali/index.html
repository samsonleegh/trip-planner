Quiet Bali Loop

Five days for three people, keeping well away from the south coast and Ubud. The loop climbs into the rice terraces and misty lake country around Munduk for a jungle summit, then canyons down a north Bali gorge. From there it heads east to snorkel off Amed and walk the Sidemen valley, and it ends with a market town and a babi guling lunch on the way to the airport.

How off-track is this? Mostly jungle, rivers and villages, with one well-known stop.
Nature & adventure 55%Villages 20%Road 25%

Jatiluwih is on most itineraries, but you walk the back trails rather than stand at the café viewpoints. Everything else gets mostly local visitors: a rainforest summit, a sacred lake you can only paddle, a river gorge, a walled Bali Aga village and the Sidemen valley. What we left out ↓

Hiking, canyoning, caves? Two adventure days: a jungle summit and a canyon.

Day 2: climb Mt Lesung (1,865 m) through rainforest above the twin lakes. It's about 5 h with 800 m of climbing. Day 3: abseil, jump and slide down a north Bali canyon. You need to be able to swim. Both have a fallback if it rains hard.

Want more? Days 4 and 5 each have an optional swap: Goa Raja, a quiet waterfall cave you can swim in, and Telaga Waja white-water rafting. Otherwise those two days stay easy.

Day by day

Times are WITA (UTC+8) · sunrise ≈ 05:45
Sat12 DecDay 1

Up into the rice bowl

Airport → Jatiluwih → Munduk, about 100 km. Two nights in the hills.

Rice terracesTrail walkLocal lunch
  1. Arrive

    Driver meets you at Ngurah Rai (DPS). Head straight north and out of the south's traffic.

  2. +2 h

    North through Tabanan. Stop for lunch in Tabanan town: ayam betutu (slow-cooked spiced chicken) or nasi campur at a warung your driver likes.

  3. 13:30

    Jatiluwih, the UNESCO-listed subak terraces. Skip the roadside café strip and walk the blue route (3.1 km) or the yellow route (2.3 km) out among the paddies and irrigation channels. Entry ≈ IDR 75–100k.

  4. +1.5 h

    Over the Bedugul pass and along the ridge above Lakes Buyan and Tamblingan to Munduk. Evenings are cool, about 18 °C.

  5. 18:30

    Dinner in Munduk village. Clove and coffee country, so try the local coffee.

If it's pouring

The terraces still look good in drizzle, but the trails get slick in a downpour. Plan B is a soak at Penatahan hot spring, 20 min south of Jatiluwih (≈ IDR 50k).

Heads up

In Dec 2025 farmers protested a crackdown on illegal buildings here, and visitor numbers dropped for weeks. It's back to normal in 2026, but a new central car park is due by end-2026, so expect some roadworks.

Sun13 DecDay 2

Jungle summit above the twin lakes

Mt Lesung, 1,865 m. About 800 m of climbing, then a paddle and a hot spring.

Rainforest hikeSummitLake canoeHot spring
  1. 06:00

    Meet your guide at the Lake Tamblingan entrance and climb Mt Lesung: 7 km and about 5 h round trip through dense, mossy rainforest to the crater rim. On a clear morning you'll see straight down to Tamblingan and Buyan lakes. Guided treks are arranged at the lake entrance (or through Munduk Wilderness, which runs trek-and-canoe trips), so have your hotel book a guide the evening before and agree the price then.

  2. 11:00

    Back at the shore, cross Lake Tamblingan by wooden paddle boat (no engines allowed) past Pura Dalem Tamblingan. Each boat takes two passengers plus the rower, so book two (≈ IDR 150–200k each). Lake entry ≈ IDR 20–30k. Bring a sarong, and don't swim: the lake is sacred.

  3. 12:30

    Lunch in Munduk.

  4. 14:00

    Optional: Banyumala twin falls, 20 min away. It's 25 min down to a pool you can swim in (≈ IDR 50k).

  5. +1 h

    Down the switchbacks to the north coast near Seririt.

  6. 16:00

    Air Panas Banjar: tiered sulphur pools with carved stone spouts, and exactly what your legs want now. ≈ IDR 45k, cash only (no ATM), open until about 17:30. Then back up to Munduk.

If it's pouring at 05:00

The Lesung trail turns to slick mud and the summit will be in cloud. Do the Munduk waterfall walk instead: Labuhan Kebo and Melanting, about 2.5 h on village paths, ≈ IDR 20k each.

Harder swap

Mt Batukaru (2,276 m), Bali's second-highest peak, is a sacred mountain that few people climb. It's 7 km with 1,200 m of climbing: 4–5 h up and 3.5 h down from Pura Luhur Batukaru, through forest all the way, so expect few views. It's 1.5 h from Munduk, so leave at 04:00 and skip the canoe and Banjar.

Mon14 DecDay 3

Down a river gorge, then the coast road east

Canyoning near Gitgit in the morning, then the dry north-east coast to Amed.

CanyoningAbseilCliff jumpsCoast road
  1. 07:30

    Check out. The canyoning operator picks you up in Munduk, or your driver takes you to the base (about 45 min).

  2. 08:30

    Canyoning: 2–4 h inside a river gorge, abseiling down waterfalls, jumping into pools and riding natural rock slides. Two good options:
    · Rindu Adventures picks up from Munduk: Gitgit canyon ≈ IDR 1.4M pp, Sambangan ≈ 1.55M pp.
    · Adventure & Spirit is ICOpro-certified: Kalimudah (beginner, jumps to 5 m, abseils to 15 m) US$145 pp, or Kerenkali (3–4 h, beginner to advanced) US$175 pp. You must be able to swim 25 m.

  3. 12:30

    Dry off and have lunch.

  4. 13:30

    Optional: Sekumpul top viewpoint, about 30 min for the view of seven falls in one gorge (≈ IDR 20k). Pay only at the official counter.

  5. +2.5 h

    East along the coast road through Tejakula. It's drier here, with lontar palms and fewer villas.

  6. 18:00

    Check in at Amed. Grilled fish on the beach.

If the river is high

Operators call trips off when the water is too high, and they offer a rebooking or refund. Get the terms in writing. Fallback: the Sekumpul guided trek down to the base of the falls and across the river (2–3 h, ≈ IDR 125–200k pp).

Tue15 DecDay 4

Reef at dawn, a walled village, the quiet valley

Amed → Tenganan → Sidemen, about 70 km.

SnorkelShipwreckBali Aga villageRice valley
  1. 06:30

    Snorkel off the beach while the water is calmest (until about 10:30). The Japanese wreck at Banyuning lies a few metres down, and Jemeluk has a coral wall. Gear ≈ IDR 50–100k.

  2. 09:30

    Breakfast and check out.

  3. 10:30

    Optional: Tirta Gangga water palace, 30 min away (≈ IDR 90k). It's popular, so keep it short.

  4. 12:00

    Tenganan Pegringsingan, one of the last walled Bali Aga villages, older than the island's Hindu-Javanese culture. Families here still weave geringsing double-ikat, which takes years per cloth. Entry is by donation; sign the guest book.

  5. 13:30

    Lunch nearby in Candidasa.

  6. +1 h

    Inland to Sidemen.

  7. 15:30

    Check in above the rice terraces. Sidemen is a weaving village too: look for songket looms in front yards. Dinner with the valley view.

Cave option

Skip Tirta Gangga and, after lunch, detour to Goa Raja in Tembuku, Bangli. It's 15 min down a mossy canyon to a big cave with a waterfall and a pool you can swim in. It's far quieter than Tukad Cepung, and best in the wet months, when the water isn't diverted to the rice fields. ≈ IDR 25k, and it adds about 1 h of driving. Don't go down in a downpour, because the canyon floods quickly.

Sea check

After a night of heavy rain, visibility at Amed drops from about 20 m to 5–10 m. If so, skip the snorkel and give Tenganan more time.

Wed16 DecDay 5

Rice valley at dawn, a market town, babi guling

Sidemen → Klungkung → airport, about 70 km.

Valley walkRoyal courtCloth marketBabi guling
  1. 06:30

    Sidemen rice-field walk with a local guide, about 2 h along the terraces and down to the Unda river. On a clear morning you'll see Agung from below. Your hotel can arrange a guide (≈ IDR 300k for the group).

  2. 09:30

    Breakfast and check out.

  3. 10:30

    Kertha Gosa in Klungkung (Semarapura), the old royal court of justice. Its ceiling is painted with heaven-and-hell panels in the Kamasan style. ≈ IDR 50k, sarong and museum included.

  4. 11:15

    Pasar Semarapura across the road, east Bali's main market for endek and songket cloth. Parts of it are still under renovation.

  5. 12:30

    Lunch: babi guling (spit-roast pork) at Mek Subur in Banjarangkan, a true local spot with a garlic-heavy rub. Or try Pande Egi in Beng, Gianyar.

  6. +1.5 h

    To the airport. Aim for a flight after about 16:00.

Rafting option

Swap Klungkung for Telaga Waja rafting: 17 km and 2.5–3 h down east Bali's wilder river, away from the busy Ayung near Ubud. It starts near Muncan, about 20 min from Sidemen. Shorten the rice walk to an hour, be on the river by 09:00, and have lunch at the finish. Trips cost from about US$37–40 pp, usually with lunch. Book with free cancellation, because trips are called off if the river floods. Aim for a flight after 16:30.

Our call

  • Book one driver for all 5 days and send them this route. Ask for an English speaker, and agree in writing on the daily hour cap, fuel, tolls and where the driver sleeps.
  • December is peak season and prices run 20–30% higher. North and east routes cost extra, and 06:00 pickups add about IDR 100k. All of that is in the range above.
  • Self-driving isn't worth it here. The roads to Munduk and Sidemen are narrow and steep, with trucks on the Gitgit bends.

Where to save or splurge

  • Canyoning is the big spend, about US$85–175 per person. The rafting option adds about US$40 each.
  • Rindu's Gitgit canyon is about US$85. Adventure & Spirit's Kerenkali is US$175 but longer and more technical.
  • Swap Wapa for a Sidemen guesthouse and you save roughly US$150 on the night.

All prices are from 2025–26 listings and news, some sources disagree, and the activity prices come from operator and booking sites. Bring cash in small notes. Most village sites don't take cards.

Before you go

Left out on purpose

Ubud, Tegallalang, Canggu, Seminyak, Kuta: the crowds you asked to avoid. Lempuyang Gate: hour-long photo queues. Batur sunrise: hundreds of hikers on one path, and a compulsory guide-association guide. Agung: you've climbed it. Trunyan: ongoing reports of boatmen demanding extra money mid-lake. Penglipuran: a polished show village. Tukad Cepung: photo queues and unlikely light beams. Goa Raja (Day 4 option) is the quiet version. Nusa Penida and West Bali / Menjangan: each needs its own 2 days.

Weather this December

Bali's weather office says a very strong El Niño is delaying the wet season. Most of Bali only starts getting rain in December, and the peak comes in Jan–Feb 2027. So expect it to be drier than a normal December, especially on the north and east coasts. Munduk's highlands stay the wettest and foggiest part of the route, which is why every outdoor stop is in the morning. Nothing big is on the Balinese calendar for 12–16 Dec (Galungan is 13 Jan 2027), though a village temple festival may pop up anywhere.
