East Java Loop

Five days out of Surabaya for three people. Each day is built around a hike, a canyon or a hot spring, with a local food stop or two between. The route is a loop: temple ruins and a sunrise summit first, then a canyon under a waterfall, then the Bromo caldera on foot, and back to the city to finish.

Your two questions

Short answers first
Mix of city and nature? Yes, about two-thirds nature, one-third city.
Nature 55%City 25%Road 20%

The city parts are Surabaya on arrival and departure (Arab quarter, spice market, Chinatown night food street, a 1910 hotel) and an evening in Malang's Dutch-era Kayutangan district. The nature days are a volcano hike past 10th-century temples, two hot springs, a canyon walk, and the Bromo crater.

Rent a car or hire a driver? Hire a car with a driver. Skip the guide.

Split three ways, a driver costs about IDR 1.1M (≈ US$65) more per person than driving yourselves, for the whole trip. You still choose every stop yourselves. The driver handles the 3 am mountain roads, the motorbike traffic and the parking, and knows which trails are closed after rain.

You won't need a full-time guide. Pay local guides on the spot where they help: the Tumpak Sewu canyon floor, and the Bromo jeep if you want one. Full comparison ↓

Day by day

Times are WIB (UTC+7) · sunrise ≈ 05:00
Sat12 DecDay 1

Land, eat, head for the hills

Surabaya → Trawas, 55 km. Stay at the foot of Mt Penanggungan.

Surabaya foodAncient templeHot spring
  1. Arrive

    Driver meets you at Juanda (SUB). Lunch at Rawon Setan (black beef soup) or Lontong Balap, the Surabaya classics.

  2. +1.5 h

    Toll road south to Trawas.

  3. 15:30

    Candi Jolotundo, a stone bathing temple built around 977 AD on Penanggungan's west slope. Spring water still runs through the spouts, and locals come to drink and bathe in it.

  4. 17:30

    Soak at Padusan hot springs in Pacet, about 30 min away. It's a local spot with sulphur pools. Then an early dinner and bed by 21:00.

Sun13 DecDay 2

Sunrise on a temple mountain

Mt Penanggungan, 1,653 m. About 1,000 m of climbing, then over to Malang.

Night hikeMajapahit ruinsHot springMalang heritage
  1. 01:30

    Start from the Tamiajeng basecamp with headlamps. It's 3–4 h up, through forest and then loose scree near the top. Arjuno-Welirang stands right across the valley.

  2. 05:00

    Sunrise at the summit. Few foreign hikers come here, and the lower slopes hold dozens of terraced Hindu-Buddhist shrines from the Majapahit era. Ask the basecamp about the side trail past Candi Kendalisodo on the way down.

  3. 09:30

    Back at basecamp. Shower and brunch.

  4. +2 h

    Drive via Pandaan to Batu. The Pacet–Cangar mountain road has been restricted since a 2025 landslide, so go the long way round.

  5. 14:00

    Cangar hot springs, natural pools in the Raden Soerjo forest park. They reopened in April 2025, with access from the Batu side only. Good for legs after the climb.

  6. 18:00

    Malang: walk the Kayutangan heritage lanes, have colonial-era ice cream at Toko Oen (since 1930), and eat Bakso President beside the railway tracks.

If it rained overnight

Scree on Penanggungan gets slippery. Your driver can ask the basecamp the evening before. Plan B is the shorter Jolotundo trail as far as the temple terraces, then turn back.

Mon14 DecDay 3

Down into the canyon

Tumpak Sewu, a 120 m semicircle of falls, then north to the Bromo rim.

CanyonRiver walkCave
  1. 05:30

    Leave Malang. 2–2.5 h to Sidomulyo, Lumajang.

  2. 08:00

    Tumpak Sewu: look out from the top viewpoint, then climb down bamboo ladders and steps to the canyon floor and wade toward the falls. Mornings are clearest and the trail is most likely to be open.

  3. 10:00

    Follow the river 20–30 min upstream to Goa Tetes, where water drips off mossy limestone into small caves. Hire a local guide here (fees ≈ IDR 50k top + 50k gorge + 20k Goa Tetes per person).

  4. 12:30

    Climb back up, change into dry clothes, and have lunch in Lumajang.

  5. +3.5 h

    Drive via Probolinggo up to Cemoro Lawang (2,200 m). It gets cold, about 10 °C at night.

  6. 18:00

    Check in on the caldera rim. Buy your Bromo e-tickets tonight if you haven't. They're online only.

Rain rule

Rangers close the canyon floor during and after heavy rain. If it's shut, you still get the top viewpoint, and you can go straight on to Bromo and arrive in daylight.

Off-track swap

Sleep in Senduro instead and catch sunrise at B29 (2,900 m), the "land above the clouds", which looks across to both Bromo and Semeru. The last stretch is by local ojek (≈ IDR 75k pp). It adds a day's worth of driving, so drop Madakaripura on Day 4 if you do it.

Tue15 DecDay 4

Walk the sand sea

Bromo on a weekday, which means cheaper tickets and fewer people. Back to Surabaya by evening.

SunriseCaldera walkSlot gorgeNight food street
  1. 03:45

    Most people take the jeep convoy to Penanjakan. The quieter way is to walk 20 min to Seruni Point / King Kong Hill for sunrise over Bromo, Batok and Semeru.

  2. 06:00

    Walk down into the caldera from Cemoro Lawang, across the sand sea and past the Hindu temple Pura Luhur Poten, then up 250 steps to the smoking crater (2,329 m). It's about 1 h each way, with no jeep needed.

  3. 08:30

    Optional: hire a jeep for the far side of the caldera, the Whispering Sands and the green Savanna ("Teletubbies Hill"). A private jeep costs ≈ IDR 550–750k and seats all three of you.

  4. 10:30

    Breakfast and check out.

  5. 11:30

    Madakaripura, a slot gorge where falls pour off the walls all around you. You'll get soaked, so rent the ponchos at the gate.

  6. +3 h

    Drive to Surabaya.

  7. 19:30

    Kya-Kya Kembang Jepun, the old Chinatown night food street. Then optionally drive across the Suramadu bridge to Madura for Bebek Sinjay fried duck.

December reality

The current at Madakaripura can be too strong for wading in the wet season. If the gate staff say no, skip it and get to Surabaya early. Sunrise at Bromo is about a coin flip in December. The caldera walk is worth doing either way, because the fog makes the sand sea look eerie.

Wed16 DecDay 5

Old port city on foot

Surabaya's north side. Use Grab or Gojek. No driver needed today.

HeritageMarketsFood
  1. 08:00

    Ampel Arab quarter: covered lanes lead to the 15th-century Sunan Ampel mosque, with dates, attar and kebuli rice stalls along the way.

  2. 09:30

    Pasar Pabean, a colonial-era spice and dried fish market. Loud and sensory.

  3. 10:30

    House of Sampoerna, a 1862 Dutch building that's now a museum. On weekdays you can watch the floor of women hand-rolling kretek at speed, and today is a weekday.

  4. 12:30

    Lunch: Sate Klopo Ondomohen (coconut-crusted satay). Then the airport, about 45 min.

Our call

  • Book a car and driver for Days 1–4. Say you want an English-speaking driver, not a tour package. That way the itinerary stays yours.
  • On Day 5, drop the driver. Grab and Gojek are cheap and everywhere in Surabaya and Malang.
  • Hire local guides on site only where they're worth it: the Tumpak Sewu canyon floor and Goa Tetes, plus the Penanggungan basecamp if you want a temple-trail guide.

Self-drive only if

  • One of you is used to driving in Southeast Asia on the left side of the road, with motorbikes all around.
  • You're happy to drop the pre-dawn starts, or to let the driver do just those legs.
  • Middle ground: self-drive on the lowland days, then hire a local car for Bromo. It saves money but adds hassle.

All prices are estimates from 2025 listings. Confirm current rates directly with the operator. Agree in writing what's included: fuel, tolls, hours, and the driver's lodging.

Before you go

Ticks stay in this browser only

Left out on purpose

We left out Ijen (blue fire, sulphur miners). It's 6+ hours past Bromo, and doing it well needs 2 more days, or flying out of Banyuwangi. Add it if you can stretch to 7 days. We also left out Semeru and Ranu Kumbolo, since the summit is closed and the lake camp needs a permit and an extra night.

Weather pattern

Mid-December is the wet season. A typical day is a clear morning, cloud building by noon, and heavy rain from about 14:00. Every outdoor stop in this plan is scheduled for the morning so the rain mostly hits while you're driving.
